Who these terms are for

Turn-Table™ is a set of science games for middle school classrooms. It is made and run by Dennis Turner through The Science Hook Lab. These terms are the rules for using it. By making a teacher account, starting a trial, or playing as a student, you agree to them.

They are short on purpose. If something is unclear, email us and a real person will answer.

Teachers

You make the account, pick a plan, and set up classes for your students. You need to be 18 or older and use Turn-Table for your teaching.

Students

Students play through their teacher's class with a nickname and a PIN. A student never pays and never needs an email address.

Your account

  • Be truthful. Use a real email address so we can reach you about your account.
  • Keep your password safe. You are responsible for what happens in your account. Tell us right away if someone else got in.
  • One teacher, one account. A membership is for one teacher. It covers up to 6 classes and 130 students. Extra sections are $15 a year.
  • Students use nicknames only. Tell your class to pick a nickname, never a full name. Turn-Table refuses names that look like a first and last name.
  • School use. Turn-Table is for school and classroom learning. Please do not use it to collect student information we did not ask for.

How we handle student data is in the Privacy and Student Data page. It is part of these terms.

The founding price

The first 100 teachers to join get the founding price. It is for yearly plans only. It stays the same every year for as long as your membership stays active. If you cancel and come back later, you pay the price that is current then.

Prices are in US dollars. Taxes, if any, are added at checkout. School and district licenses are priced separately. Email us for a quote.

The 7-day free trial

Every new teacher gets 7 days to try everything. A card is needed to start the trial, but nothing is charged today.

  • The trial lasts 7 days from the day you start it.
  • If you do nothing, your card is charged automatically when the 7 days end, for the plan you picked. The price and renewal date are shown to you on the card page before you start.
  • If you cancel before the 7 days end, you are never charged.
  • One free trial per teacher.

Billing and renewals

Plans renew automatically until you cancel. Please read this part closely.

  • Yearly plans are charged once a year, on the day your trial ends and every year after that. We do not send separate bills.
  • Monthly plans are charged every month on the same day.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe through The Science Hook Lab. We never see or store your full card number.
  • If a payment fails, we may try your card again. If it still fails, your account turns read-only: you can still see your classes and scores, but you cannot start class games or make new classes. Fix your payment and it unlocks again.
  • If we ever change the price of a plan, we will email you before it takes effect. You can cancel before the new price starts.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel whenever you want. No phone call, no reason needed.

  1. Cancel in your account's billing settings, or email dennisturner@thesciencehooklab.com from your account email and we will cancel it for you.
  2. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. After that, it does not renew and you are not charged again.
  3. Your classes and scores stay put so you can come back. Ask us any time and we will delete them.

Money back questions are answered on the Refund Policy page.

The free version

If your trial ends and you do not subscribe, or you cancel, your students keep a free version: Speed Find, Cell Shop and Moonwatch (first levels), with a limited number of plays each day. Everything else, including Pre-Tests and Post-Tests and Class Games, needs a paid plan. We may change what the free version includes.

Using Turn-Table

Please don't:

  • Share your login or sell access to other people.
  • Try to break in, overload the site, or read another teacher's data.
  • Copy the games, art or questions to build a competing product.
  • Put harmful, rude or private information in nicknames or feedback messages.

We can lock an account that breaks these rules. If we do, we will tell you why.

Your data and our work

Your class data is yours. Your students' scores and your quiz boards belong to you and your school. We use them only to run Turn-Table for you.

The games are ours. The games, art, questions and code belong to The Science Hook Lab. You can use them in your classroom, but you can't copy or resell them.

Turn-Table™ and The Science Hook Lab are names we use for our products.

Limits

We work hard to keep Turn-Table running, but we can't promise it will always be online or free of mistakes. It is provided "as is." Please keep your own copy of anything you can't afford to lose, such as a gradebook.

To the most the law allows, our responsibility to you is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the problem. Nothing here takes away rights you have under the law. These terms follow the laws of the State of New York.

Turn-Table is a study and practice tool. Its scores are not a replacement for your own grading judgment.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what you pay or how student data is used, we will email you before it takes effect.
