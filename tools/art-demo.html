Turn-Table art kit

Sprites

Art.sprite(name,{size,color,level,tone,kind}) for HTML, Art.g(name,{x,y,size}) inside a game's SVG.

Scenes

Art.scene(el,'sky') gives a game area its backdrop. Art.backdrop(kind,w,h) draws the same thing inside an SVG board (below).

Effects

Bursts, sparkles, glow, pulse, pop and shake. Art.good(el) and Art.bad(el) are the usual right and wrong reactions.

Counter and tweens

Art.count(el,to) rolls a number. Art.tween({from,to,dur,ease,step}) drives anything; easings below.

Drag and drop

Art.drag({root,items,targets,accept,onDrop}). Drag a particle into the atom, or tap a particle then tap a spot. Keyboard: Tab to a particle, Enter, arrow keys, Enter. Valid spots glow green; wrong drops bounce back.
