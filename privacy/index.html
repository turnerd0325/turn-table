Privacy and student data

Your students' data stays yours.

Turn-Table was built by a classroom teacher for classroom teachers. Students play with a nickname and a PIN, only their own teacher sees their results, and nothing on the student side is used for ads.

Last updated November 22, 2026Questions: dennisturner@thesciencehooklab.com

Scores, Pre/Post-Tests, progress charts and feedback messages.

  • Their own teacherYes, only them

Only you see your students

Every request is checked on our server. A teacher can only open their own classes and students.

Nicknames only

No student email, real name, birthday, photo or location. Students never type their full name.

No ads or trackers for students

Student screens load no ad pixels, analytics or outside scripts. Students never see an ad.

We never sell data

Not student data, not teacher data. Student data is never used for marketing.

Locked down

Encrypted connections, scrambled PINs, lockouts after wrong guesses, and no direct database access.

Delete any time

Remove a student and all of their scores from your dashboard in one click.

Students: use a nickname. Never your full name.

Teachers, please tell your class before they make accounts. A nickname keeps students private even if a screen is projected or a classmate looks over. First names and initials are fine if your school allows it, but full names are not.

What we collect, and what we don't

We collect the least we can and still run the games and show you your students' progress.

From students

  • A nickname they make up
  • A 4-digit PIN, stored scrambled so nobody can read it
  • The class they joined with your class code
  • Game results: scores, stars, levels, Pre/Post-Test scores, elements they missed, and when they last played
  • Feedback messages they choose to send to you

Badges, play time and settings are saved only on the student's own device.

From teachers

  • Your name (shown to your students), school (optional) and email
  • Your password, stored scrambled by our sign-in provider. We can't see it
  • Your classes, game settings and quiz boards
  • Your plan and trial dates
  • How you found us (for example, which ad or post), if that was in the link you used

What we never collect from students

Real names, email addresses, birthdays, photos, voice, location, contacts, or anything from their Google account. Students don't sign in with Google or any outside account.

Payments are handled by Stripe through The Science Hook Lab, the company behind Turn-Table. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To run the games, save progress and let students pick up where they left off.
  • To show you your students' scores, growth and the elements they keep missing.
  • To run your account: sign-in, your trial and plan, and emails about your account.
  • To fix bugs and make the games better. We look at overall patterns, like how many students finish a level.

We don't build profiles of students, we don't use student data for ads, and we don't use it to train AI.

Ads measurement on teacher pages only

We advertise Turn-Table to teachers on Facebook and Instagram. To know which ads work, the teacher pages use the Meta Pixel: the About page, teacher sign-in and sign-up, and the teacher dashboard.

  • It records steps like "visited the page," "made an account," "started a trial" and "made a class." It does not send names, emails, class names or any student information.
  • Meta may receive your browser details and IP address and set a cookie in your browser.
  • We turned off Meta's automatic features, so it can't read forms or record buttons on its own.
  • It never runs on student screens. The pixel file isn't even downloaded while a student is signed in.

You can limit this in your Facebook ad preferences or with your browser's tracking or ad blocking settings. Turn-Table works the same either way.

Security

  • Encrypted connections. Every page and every save uses HTTPS. Our database provider encrypts stored data.
  • Scrambled PINs and passwords. Student PINs and teacher passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), not as readable text.
  • Lockouts. Too many wrong PINs locks that nickname for a while, so nobody can guess their way in.
  • Checked on the server. The database can't be read directly. Every request goes through a checked function that confirms who is asking and that the class or student is theirs.
  • Account controls. Teachers can reset a student's PIN, lock an account, or delete a student.

No system is perfect. If student data is ever exposed, we will tell the affected teachers and schools promptly and within the time New York Education Law 2-d requires, and explain what happened and what we're doing about it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Delete a student yourself. In your dashboard, open the student and tap Delete. Their account and every score are removed for good, right away.
  • Delete a whole class or your account. Email us from your account email. We delete it within 30 days and confirm when it's done.
  • If your trial ends or you cancel, your classes and scores stay so you can come back. Ask us any time and we'll delete them.
  • End of the school year? Delete the students you no longer teach, or email us and we'll clear a class for you.

FERPA, COPPA and New York Education Law 2-d

Turn-Table is designed so you can use it within your school's student privacy rules.

FERPA

Your students' records belong to you and your school. We use them only to provide Turn-Table to you, and we don't share them with anyone else.

COPPA

We don't ask children for personal information: no names, emails or birthdays. Teachers and schools set up the classes, for school use only.

NY Ed Law 2-d

We never sell student data or use it for marketing. It's encrypted in transit, used only to run the service, and you can have it deleted.

Turn-Table isn't certified by an outside privacy program. If your district needs a data privacy agreement, a Parents' Bill of Rights supplement or a vendor form, email us and we'll work through it with you.

For parents and students

Turn-Table is a school tool. Students play under their teacher's class, with a nickname instead of a name. Parents who want to see or delete their child's data can ask their child's teacher, or email us. We'll work with the teacher to confirm the student and handle the request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how student data is used, we'll tell teachers by email before it takes effect. We will never start using student data for advertising.
