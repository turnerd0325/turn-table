Laser Maze MS-PS4-2

Light up every crystal with the laser! Mirrors reflect light, glass lets it through, and black material soaks it up. Use as few pieces as you can to earn 3 stars.

  1. Pick a level below.
  2. Drag a piece into the maze (or click a piece, then click a square). Click a placed mirror to flip it.
  3. Light every crystal and keep the beam off the alarms. The beam updates as you build.
