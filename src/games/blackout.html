Blackout! MS-PS3-6

The city has gone dark! Wire batteries, bulbs and switches so electric current carries energy to every lamp. Use as few pieces as you can to earn 3 stars.

  1. Pick a level below.
  2. Drag along the dashed lines with the Wire tool to build the circuit. Click a switch to open or close it. Click a battery to flip it.
  3. Press Test the city. Every test must pass: lights on, and lights still on when a bulb burns out!
