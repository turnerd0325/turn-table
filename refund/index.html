Free trial: no charge if you cancel

Your 7-day trial costs $0. A card is needed to start it, but we do not charge it during the trial.

  • Cancel before the 7 days end and you are never charged. There is nothing to refund.
  • Do nothing and your card is charged automatically when the trial ends, for the plan you picked.

Tip: when you start the trial, put a reminder on your calendar for day 6.

Yearly plans

If you are charged for a yearly plan (or a yearly renewal) and you did not mean to keep it, email us within 14 days of the charge and we will refund the whole payment. No hoops, no reason needed.

After 14 days, a yearly plan is not refunded, but you can cancel at any time so it will not renew. You keep full access until the end of the year you paid for.

Monthly plans

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for, and you will not be charged again. We do not refund a month that has already started, but if you were charged by mistake, email us within 14 days and we will make it right.

When we always refund

  • You were charged twice for the same plan.
  • You were charged after you cancelled in time.
  • Turn-Table did not work for you and we could not fix it after you told us.
  • A renewal happened and you tell us within 14 days that you did not realize it would renew.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email dennisturner@thesciencehooklab.com from the email on your account. Tell us which plan.
  2. We reply within 2 school days to confirm.
  3. Approved refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 business days.

Founding price members: if you cancel and get a refund, your founding spot is released. If you come back later, the price that is current at that time applies.

How to cancel

Cancel in your account's billing settings, or email us from your account email and we will cancel it for you. Cancelling stops the next charge. It does not delete your classes or scores. You can come back whenever you like, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time.

The full rules are in the Terms of Use.

School and district licenses

School licenses are quoted and billed separately, so your refund terms are written in your quote or invoice. If none are written there, the yearly rules above apply.

Need a refund or have a question?

Email Dennis Turner. We usually reply within 2 school days.
